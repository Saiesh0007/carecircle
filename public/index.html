Everything a care circle needs

Never miss a dose

Daily timeline with one-tap Taken / Skip. Missed doses alert the family by email automatically.

AI care assistant

Ask in Hindi or English, by voice or text. Paste a prescription and it fills the schedule for you.

Predicts missed doses

A risk model learns from past weeks and warns you which dose is likely to be missed today.

Shared and secure

Family joins with a care code and passcode, or signs in with Google / GitHub. The elder stays at the centre.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create a care circle

    Add the elder's health profile and choose a shared passcode.

  2. 2

    Invite the family

    Share the care code. Everyone picks a role: elder, caretaker or family.

  3. 3

    Stay in sync

    Track doses and visits together; reminders and AI insights do the rest.

CareCircle supports care coordination and is not a substitute for medical advice. In an emergency call 112.
